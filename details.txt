   ↓
Can submit complaints

ADMIN
   ↓
Can review complaints
Can change complaint status
Can mark complaint "Unreasonable"

MAIN ADMIN × 2
   ↓
Full control
Can approve/reject Admin applications
Can manage admins
Can review unreasonable complaints
